# Stopping-depth signed Buchstab re-entry audit — 2026-09-17

## Status
- Riemann hypothesis: OPEN.
- Resolution-stopping branches: exact and still useful as bookkeeping.
- First-divergence local parity contraction: closed by parity deferral.
- New result: the full ordered rough-tail recursion is an exact **signed Buchstab recursion** for the Möbius exponential sum.

## 1. Rough Möbius exponential sum
For `y>=1` define on a dyadic interval

\[
R(D,y;\alpha)
:=
\sum_{\substack{D<n\le2D\\P^-(n)>y}}
\mu(n)e(\alpha n),
\]

with the convention `P^-(1)=+infinity` when needed for endpoint bookkeeping.

Let `z>y`.

Every squarefree integer counted by `R(D,y;alpha)` either has no prime factor in `(y,z]`, or has a unique least prime factor `p in (y,z]`.

In the latter case write

\[
n=pm,
\qquad P^-(m)>p.
\]

Since `n` is squarefree,

\[
\mu(pm)=-\mu(m).
\]

Therefore the exact dyadic recursion is

\[
\boxed{
R(D,y;\alpha)
=
R(D,z;\alpha)
-
\sum_{y<p\le z}
R(D/p,p;p\alpha),
}
\]

with the obvious exact integer endpoint convention on `(D/p,2D/p]`.

Classification:

`SIGNED_BUCHSTAB_MOBIUS_PHASE_RECURSION`.

This is the Möbius-weighted exponential-sum version of the standard least-prime Buchstab decomposition.

## 2. Iteration and ordered prime activation
Iterating the recursion chooses ordered primes

\[
p_1<p_2<\cdots<p_j
\]

and maps

\[
(D,H,\alpha)
\mapsto
\left(
\frac{D}{p_1\cdots p_j},
\frac{H}{p_1\cdots p_j},
(p_1\cdots p_j)\alpha
\right)
\]

when `alpha~1/H`.

Each activation contributes one minus sign to the branch amplitude.

Thus after depth `j` the branch coefficient is

\[
(-1)^j
\]

multiplying a rough Möbius tail at the scaled variables.

## 3. Resolution stopping rule
Let

\[
q_j=p_1\cdots p_j.
\]

Stopping at the first `j` for which

\[
q_j\ge H
\]

is exactly the previous resolution-stopping rule.

At the stopped node,

\[
H_j=H/q_j\le1.
\]

Equivalently, the scaled Fourier band has width/order-one period length, which explains why Parseval gives the correct branchwise scale without any Möbius cancellation.

Classification:

`RESOLUTION_STOPPING_AS_SIGNED_BUCHSTAB_STOPPING_TIME`.

## 4. What is standard and what is phase-adapted
Standard:
- least-prime decomposition;
- ordered rough cofactors;
- iteration of the Buchstab identity;
- alternating signs induced by Möbius under prime removal.

Phase-adapted to the reciprocal problem:
- the stopping boundary is not a fixed roughness level but the dynamically generated Fourier resolution `H`;
- iteration stops when the cumulative dilation turns the shrinking band into an order-one band;
- every leaf is individually at diagonal band scale by periodicity.

Therefore no novelty claim should be attached to the tree identity itself.

## 5. Why the recursion alone does not prove contraction
If one applies triangle inequality to the signed Buchstab recursion, one obtains a sum of branch norms and loses the cancellations between branches.

If one squares first and then uses Cauchy/absolute estimates on branch pairs, the unresolved quantity is precisely the cross-branch interference isolated in the stopping audit.

Moreover:
- common-prefix stripping gives only self-similar rescaling;
- finite first-divergence signs cancel pairwise in `mu(n)mu(m)`;
- finite-order label marginalization re-enters the CRT tensor/restricted-Möbius barriers.

Thus the standard recursion is bookkeeping, not yet the needed contraction theorem.

## 6. Transfer-operator viewpoint
Formally define the signed prime-dilation operator

\[
(\mathcal T f)(D,y,\alpha)
:=
-\sum_{p>y} f(D/p,p,p\alpha)
\]

with the prime range truncated according to the dyadic support and the chosen next roughness boundary.

The stopped expansion is a finite tree generated by repeated applications of `T`, terminated when the cumulative prime product reaches `H`.

The proof problem is therefore not to derive the recursion but to establish a norm statement of the form

\[
\|\text{sum of stopped leaves}\|_{L^2(I_H)}^2
\lesssim
\sum_{\text{leaves}}\|\text{leaf}\|_{L^2(I_H)}^2
\]

up to `D^epsilon`-type loss.

This is an almost-orthogonality statement for the **signed stopped Buchstab tree**.

No such estimate has been proved here.

## 7. Literature boundary
Classical Buchstab theory and modern weighted-sieve work explicitly use the least-prime recursion for rough numbers and its iterates. Weighted rough-number analyses also organize contributions by the number/order of activated primes.

The present audit therefore treats the recursive decomposition as standard background.

What has not been identified in the present literature search is a theorem giving the exact phase-dependent almost-orthogonality required for the shrinking band `I_H=[1/H,sqrt(2)/H]` under this stopping rule.

Absence from the present search is not a novelty proof.

## 8. Updated live question
The stopping route now has one precise unresolved statement:

> Are the leaves of the signed Buchstab tree, stopped when their cumulative prime product first exceeds the Fourier resolution `H`, almost orthogonal in the reciprocal mesoscopic band at total diagonal scale?

A positive answer must use something beyond:
- branchwise Parseval;
- common-prefix divisibility;
- fixed-depth parity;
- static prime-label tensors;
- triangle inequality over Buchstab branches.

A negative reduction to a known sieve-parity barrier would close the stopping-tree route.
