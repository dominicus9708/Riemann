## 1. Extract the prime-square layer

Let

\[
\Theta_m:=\vartheta(m^2),\qquad U_m:=\vartheta(m),
\]

and

\[
L_m:=\Theta_{m+1}-\Theta_m.
\]

The k=2 prime-power contribution to the square-shell reserve is

\[
A_m^{(2)}
:=L_m\frac{U_m+U_{m+1}}2.

The higher prime-power layers k>=3 contribute only lower order to the cumulative M^3 scale, because

\[
\psi(m^2)-\vartheta(m^2)-\vartheta(m)

Consequently

\[
\sum_{m<M}\bigl(A_m-A_m^{(2)}\bigr)=o(M^3),
\]

so the full leading 2/3 coefficient already comes from the prime-square layer.

## 2. Exact cross-scale oriented flux

Define

\[
W_m^{(2)}
:=\Theta_{m+1}U_m-\Theta_mU_{m+1}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
A_m^{(2)}
=\frac12\Delta(\Theta U)_m
+\frac12W_m^{(2)}.
}
\]

Since

\[
\Delta U_m=U_{m+1}-U_m,
\]

we can rewrite the oriented term as

\[
\boxed{
W_m^{(2)}=U_mL_m-\Theta_m\Delta U_m.
}
\]

This exposes the two cross-scale channels directly:
- `shell inflow`: \(U_mL_m\), primes entering near scale m^2 weighted by cumulative root-scale prime mass;
- `root activation cost`: \(\Theta_m\Delta U_m\), paid only when m+1 itself is prime.

Because

\[
\Delta U_m=
\begin{cases}
\log(m+1),&m+1\text{ prime},\\
0,&m+1\text{ composite},
\end{cases}
\]

we have the exact sign structure

\[
\boxed{
m+1\text{ composite}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad W_m^{(2)}=U_mL_m\ge0.}
\]

Negative local oriented flux can occur only at root-prime activation steps.

## 3. Cumulative asymptotic

Summing the exact flux gives

\[
\sum_{m<M}W_m^{(2)}
=
\sum_{m<M}U_mL_m
-
\sum_{q\le M\atop q\text{ prime}}\Theta_{q-1}\log q
+O(1).
\]

By PNT,

\[
U_m\sim m,\qquad \Theta_m\sim m^2.
\]

The first term has asymptotic

\[
\sum_{m<M}U_mL_m\sim\frac23M^3,
\]

while weighted prime partial summation gives

\[
\sum_{q\le M}\Theta_{q-1}\log q\sim\sum_{q\le M}q^2\log q\sim\frac13M^3.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{m<M}W_m^{(2)}\sim\frac13M^3.
}
\]

Together with \(\Theta_MU_M\sim M^3\), this yields

\[
\sum_{m<M}A_m^{(2)}\sim\frac23M^3.
\]

Thus the leading prime-square reserve is a net positive difference between a shell-inflow channel of coefficient 2/3 and a sparse root-activation channel of coefficient 1/3.
